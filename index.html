WHO ARE WE?

Bringing Unmatched Clarity & Detail To Every Window

Founded in 2012 in North Texas, ClearView Pro was built on a single premise: traditional window cleaning with soap and squeegees leaves a microscopic chemical film that attracts dust like a magnet.

We invested in multi-stage Reverse Osmosis and De-Ionization (RO/DI) filtration rigs that produce 99.9% pure water. When fed through carbon-fiber water-fed poles, pure water acts as a natural solvent, absorbing dirt, hard water minerals, and pollen without any chemicals. Best of all, it dries 100% crystal-clear and streak-free!
